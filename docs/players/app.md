# The companion app

The OpenPak app puts your account, friends, invitations and saves on your phone. It is one
codebase for Android and iOS; **Android** builds are published, iOS is not yet.

Download: [OpenPak app releases](https://github.com/openpak/app/releases/latest) (also linked
from [openpak.org/downloads](https://openpak.org/downloads)). New releases install over the
previous one.

## What it does

- **Sign in** with your OpenPak account (and its two-step code), or create one. A forgotten
  password is reset by email.
- **Friends** with presence: who is online and what they are playing. Accept or decline
  requests, and unblock people you blocked.
- **Invitations** waiting for you, with decline. Opening the inbox does not use invitations
  up, so your console still receives them.
- **Titles**: the latest news, which games have a server, who is online on each network, and a
  way to request the next game.
- **Saves**: your usage, each game's versions to restore, download or delete, and your own
  storage.
- **Chat** with friends.
- **Account**: details, avatar (a built-in one or your own photo), password, email, two-step
  sign-in, linked consoles (link a Wii or DS, unlink any), signed-in devices, and deleting the
  account.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app tells you about things like new friend requests. The app
uses a push token for your device to do this; see [privacy](privacy.md).

## Crash reports

If the app crashes, it saves a report on the phone (the last five) and offers to send it, on
the spot or at the next launch. Nothing is sent until you choose **Send**, or **Always send**
(which you can change on the account page).
