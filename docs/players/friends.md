# Friends and chat

OpenPak has **one friend list across every platform**. A friend you add from a Switch is also
your friend on a Wii U, in an emulator, on the website and in the app.

## Friend codes

Each linked console gets a friend code. Switch codes look like `SW-1234-5678-9012` (twelve hex
digits). When you type one, case does not matter and the `SW-` prefix and the dashes are
optional. Your codes are listed on your [account page](https://openpak.org/account).

Wii and DS games show their own WFC friend codes; those are what you type to
[link a Wii or DS](account.md#link-a-console-or-emulator).

## Adding friends

- **On the website:** [openpak.org/account/friends](https://openpak.org/account/friends) →
  **Add by friend code** → **Send request**.
- **In the app:** the Friends tab.
- **On a console:** the console's own friend screens, which OpenPak answers.

The other person accepts or declines, and the friendship shows up on both sides. You can
remove a friend at any time.

## Presence

Friends see whether you are **online**, **in a game** (and which one, on which console or
emulator) or **offline**. Presence works on the Switch but is still partial; offline friends
stay in your list.

## Blocking

**Block** stops someone from sending you requests, messages or invitations. Blocked accounts
are listed on the friends page with **Unblock**.

## Chat

There is one conversation with each friend, on every surface: the website
([openpak.org/account/chat](https://openpak.org/account/chat)), the app and the console. A
message sent on the website is the one their console shows. You can only message friends.

## Game invitations

Invitations sent from a game are delivered and listed at
[openpak.org/account/invitations](https://openpak.org/account/invitations) and in the app.
Reading them there does not use them up, so the console still receives them.

!!! note
    Invitations are partial during the alpha: they arrive, but joining a game from an
    invitation does not always work yet.
