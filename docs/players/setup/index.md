# Setup guides

Pick the console or emulator you want to connect. Each guide says what you need, how to
install it, how to undo it, and what is not ready yet.

Read [before you start](../before-you-start.md) first, and [create your account](../account.md).

## Consoles

| Console | What you need | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nintendo Switch | Atmosphère custom firmware and **OpenPak Setup** | [Switch](switch.md) |
| Wii U | Aroma and the **Inkay** module for OpenPak | [Wii U](wiiu.md) |
| 3DS | Luma3DS and **nimbus** for OpenPak | [3DS](3ds.md) |
| Wii | The **Wii WFC patcher** for OpenPak | [Wii](wii.md) |
| DS | The **DS WFC patcher** for OpenPak | [DS](ds.md) |
| Xbox, Xbox 360, PS3 | Not ready for players yet | |

## Emulators

OpenPak keeps its own emulator forks. They boot the real console firmware where they can and
go online through OpenPak the way a real console would.

| Emulator | Console | Based on | Ready? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Harbor](emulators/harbor.md) | Switch | Eden | Alpha, test builds published |
| [Quay](emulators/quay.md) | Wii U | Cemu | Not ready yet |
| [Marina](emulators/marina.md) | Wii | Dolphin | Not ready yet |
| [Azahar for OpenPak](emulators/azahar.md) | 3DS | Azahar | Not ready yet |
| [melonDS for OpenPak](emulators/melonds.md) | DS | melonDS | Not ready yet |
| [Wharf](emulators/wharf.md) | Xbox 360 | Xenia Edge | Not ready yet |
| [Berth](emulators/berth.md) | Xbox | xemu | Not ready yet |
| [Pier](emulators/pier.md) | PlayStation 3 | RPCS3 | Not ready yet |

Downloads for everything that has a public release are collected at
[openpak.org/downloads](https://openpak.org/downloads).
