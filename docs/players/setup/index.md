# Setup guides

Pick the console or emulator you want to connect. Each guide says what you need, how to
install it, how to undo it, and what is not ready yet.

Read [before you start](../before-you-start.md) first, and [create your account](../account.md).

## Consoles

| Console | What you need | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nintendo Switch | Atmosphère custom firmware and **OpenPak Switch Patcher** | [Switch](switch.md) |
| Wii U | Aroma and the **OpenPak Wii U Patcher** | [Wii U](wiiu.md) |
| 3DS | Luma3DS and the **OpenPak 3DS Patcher** | [3DS](3ds.md) |
| Wii | The **OpenPak Wii Patcher** | [Wii](wii.md) |
| DS | The **OpenPak DS Patcher** | [DS](ds.md) |
| Xbox | DNS setting; **OpenPak Xbox Patcher** not available yet ([repository](https://github.com/openpak/xbox-patcher)) | [Xbox](xbox.md) |
| Xbox 360 | DNS setting; **OpenPak Xbox 360 Patcher** not available yet ([repository](https://github.com/openpak/x360-patcher)) | [Xbox 360](xbox360.md) |
| PlayStation 3 | DNS setting; **OpenPak PS3 Patcher** not available yet ([repository](https://github.com/openpak/ps3-patcher)) | [PlayStation 3](ps3.md) |
| PlayStation 4 | Nothing to set up yet | [PlayStation 4](ps4.md) |

## Emulators

OpenPak keeps its own emulator forks. They boot the real console firmware where they can and
go online through OpenPak the way a real console would.

| Emulator | Console | Based on | Ready? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Harbor](emulators/harbor.md) | Switch | Eden | Alpha, test builds published |
| [Quay](emulators/quay.md) | Wii U | Cemu | Early: runs games, no online play tried |
| [Marina](emulators/marina.md) | Wii | Dolphin | Not ready yet, no download |
| [Azahar for OpenPak](emulators/azahar.md) | 3DS | Azahar | Not ready yet, no download |
| [melonDS for OpenPak](emulators/melonds.md) | DS | melonDS | Not ready yet, no download |
| [Wharf](emulators/wharf.md) | Xbox 360 | Xenia Edge | Experimental: dashboard sign-in works, no download |
| [Berth](emulators/berth.md) | Xbox | xemu | Experimental: dashboard sign-in works, no download |
| [Pier](emulators/pier.md) | PlayStation 3 | RPCS3 | Experimental: sign-in works, test nightly |

Downloads for everything that has a public release are collected at
[openpak.org/downloads](https://openpak.org/downloads).
