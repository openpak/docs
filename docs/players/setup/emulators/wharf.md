# Wharf (Xbox 360)

Wharf is OpenPak's Xbox 360 emulator, built on Xenia Edge. It boots the console's own
dashboard and connects it to OpenPak's Xbox 360 services.

## Status

**Not ready yet.** The dashboard's Xbox Live connection test passes against OpenPak, but
sign-in does not complete yet.

## Downloads

- **Release:** [github.com/openpak/wharf/releases/latest](https://github.com/openpak/wharf/releases/latest),
  once a tagged release exists.
- **Nightly:** the rolling [`nightly` pre-release](https://github.com/openpak/wharf/releases/tag/nightly),
  rebuilt when the source changes. It is untested: use it to follow progress, not to play.

## Sign in to OpenPak

There is no way to sign in to OpenPak from Wharf yet: dashboard sign-in stops before it
finishes.

## Known limits

- No sign-in, so no online play.
- You supply the console's system files and your own games.

Issues: [github.com/openpak/wharf/issues](https://github.com/openpak/wharf/issues). Say which
build you used (release or nightly) and what you saw.
