# Pier (PlayStation 3)

Pier is OpenPak's PS3 emulator, built on RPCS3. It boots the console's own XMB.

## Status

**Not ready yet.** Sign-in works and friends' online status comes through, but online game
sessions do not work yet and part of the setup is still a manual step.

## Downloads

- **Release:** [github.com/openpak/pier/releases/latest](https://github.com/openpak/pier/releases/latest),
  once a tagged release exists.
- **Nightly:** the rolling [`nightly` pre-release](https://github.com/openpak/pier/releases/tag/nightly),
  rebuilt when the source changes. It is untested: use it to follow progress, not to play.

## Sign in to OpenPak

Use the XMB's own sign-in form, with your OpenPak email address and password.

## Known limits

- Online game sessions do not work yet.
- Part of the setup is still a manual step.
- You supply the firmware and your own games.

Issues: [github.com/openpak/pier/issues](https://github.com/openpak/pier/issues). Say which
build you used (release or nightly) and what you saw.
