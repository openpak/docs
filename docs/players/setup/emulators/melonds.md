# melonDS for OpenPak (DS)

A fork of melonDS with a Nintendo WFC redirect to OpenPak, cloud saves for `.sav` files and the
shared OpenPak menu.

## Status

**Not ready yet.** Not run against a game. This fork is transitional: a successor is planned,
and no build is advertised from it until then.

## Downloads

- **Release:** [github.com/openpak/melonds/releases/latest](https://github.com/openpak/melonds/releases/latest),
  once a tagged release exists.
- **Nightly:** the rolling [`nightly` pre-release](https://github.com/openpak/melonds/releases/tag/nightly),
  rebuilt when the source changes. It is untested: use it to follow progress, not to play.

## Sign in to OpenPak

1. Sign in from the **OpenPak** menu (left of Help).
2. Turn on **Connect Nintendo WFC to OpenPak** in the Wi-Fi settings.

You supply your own ROMs, and for firmware boot, the firmware and BIOS from a DS you own.

## Known limits

- Untested against any game.
- The fork retires once its successor lands.
