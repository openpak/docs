# Privacy and account deletion

The full texts are the [Privacy policy](https://openpak.org/privacy) and the
[Terms](https://openpak.org/terms). They are what applies; this page is a short guide.

## What OpenPak keeps, in short

- **Your account:** email address, a password hash (OpenPak cannot read your password), your
  handle and display name, and the country and date of birth consoles ask for.
- **What you use:** linked consoles, friends, blocks, invitations, chat messages, presence and
  play history, cloud saves, help requests and crash reports you choose to send.
- **Network data:** your IP address reaches every server you connect to. In many games the
  console connects directly to the other players, so they can see your address, as on the
  original networks.

OpenPak uses no analytics, advertising or tracking, and does not sell or share personal data
for marketing.

## Your choices

- Your profile (display name, avatar, bio) is visible to other players. Edit it on your
  [account page](https://openpak.org/account).
- Revoke console links and sign out devices on your account page.
- Disconnect your own save storage at any time.
- Turn app notifications off in the app or on the phone.
- Crash reports are only sent when you say yes.

## Delete your account

1. Sign in at [openpak.org](https://openpak.org) and open your
   [account page](https://openpak.org/account), or open **Account** in the app.
2. Choose **Delete account**, tick the confirmation, and confirm with your password (and your
   two-step code or a recovery code, if you use one).

From that moment the account is closed: every session is signed out and nobody can sign in to
it. Your friends, links and saves go with the account.

During the alpha, the final erasure is done by hand by the team, within one month. Some data
(cloud saves, chat messages, help requests and crash reports) is not yet erased by the same
step; the [Privacy policy](https://openpak.org/privacy) says exactly what is erased and how to
ask for the rest to be removed.

## Questions and requests

Write to [contact@openpak.org](mailto:contact@openpak.org) for a copy of your data, a
correction, or anything else about your data.
